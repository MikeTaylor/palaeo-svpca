Paris 2006
12th - 16th September 2006

The 54th SVPCA and the 15th SPPC was held this year in Paris, the institutional organiser being the Société géologique de France, and the venue the Ecole Nationale des Mines (National School of Mines), on the boulevard Saint-Michel in the heart of the "Latin quarter" of Paris.


Field Trip


The field trip to Meudon, in the southern suburbs of Paris, was on the morning of Saturday, September 16th.
