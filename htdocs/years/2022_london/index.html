London 2022
21st - 23rd September 2022

The meeting is the re-arranged 2020 meeting, so most details remain the same, including a pre-meeting workshop on phylogenetic comparative methods, a symposium on the contributions of the British fossil record to palaeobiology, and the auction and annual dinner venues.


Circulars will not be sent out this year, so check svpca2020.com and follow us @svpca2020 on Twitter for information.


The meeting will not be fully hybrid, but there are plans to live-stream some components of the meeting. Registration and abstract submission will open shortly so please regularly check the website svpca2020.com for updates.
