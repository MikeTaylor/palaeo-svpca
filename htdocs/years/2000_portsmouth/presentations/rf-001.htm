People come into vertebrate palaeontology by a number of different routes. Geology, Zoology and the most common, but there are others - computer science for one. As an architect, my route into the discipline is unusual.

However, all these different backgrounds give differing perspectives and viewpoints on the subject.

Architects don't lay bricks or install plumbing themselves. They provide the information needed to allow the models they have constructed in their minds to be built, using a set of conventions- drawings, specification and schedules - to communicate to other members of the construction team. Palaeontologist also structure data formally as papers, which are descriptions of aspects of the model held in the mind of the palaeontologist. These may be drawn on as information sources in creating reconstructions of ancient lives. Science can be described as a process of building models, tested by experiment and evidence to show that they are consistent and coherent. We are trying to build a consistent and coherent model of the world.
