Palaeontology and Architecture are very similar disciplines. Unlike physics and engineering, which operate within a tight set of constraints, architecture and palaeontology are synthetic disciplines, in that they bring together a huge range of inputs - from engineering, materials science, psychology, building regulations, finance, planning law, aesthetics, style and so on for architecture, from geology, biology, physiology, anatomy, ecology, biomechanics, mathematics, statistics and so on for palaeontology - to build their models. An analogy can be drawn with the problem of crossing a river.
