Cladistics is better Science because it is Objective
Cladistics is a way of finding patterns in large amounts of data has as a method made a valuable contribution to phylogeny and taxonomy. But there are some problems in the way it is used, and the significance given to its' findings. [cladistics] It may be a mistake to rely on subjective impressions, but I get a strong sense in talking to some people in the palaeontological community that cladistics is considered to be 'better' science than 'traditional' methods because it is 'objective'.

I have a problem with this assumption.
