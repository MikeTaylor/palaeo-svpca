The sets of propulsive limbs need to be rigidly connected to reduce the energy needed to keep moving in the same direction. Plesiosaurs do this by shortening the back, thus lengthening the neck.
At the same time, the large girdles - needed in any case for attaching the huge muscles used for propulsion - are joined by a solid basket of gastralia, forming in effect a plastron similar to that of turtle in its mechanical function. The dorsal spine is straight and in compression. A series of 15 dorsal vertebrae can be picked up by applying relatively light pressure to each end of the row.
