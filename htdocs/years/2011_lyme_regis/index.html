Accommodation


Delegates will be expected to make their own arrangement for accomodation. There is a wide range available in the area, including B&Bs, hotels, holiday lets and caravans. Although the town will still be busy in mid-September, a lot of the accommodation is booked at short notice. There will be no problem with finding accommodation if delegates book well in advance.
