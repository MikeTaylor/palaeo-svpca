London 2020

The meeting for this year has been cancelled as a consequence of the Covid 19 outbreak.


The organisers contacted those on the mailing list asking for their views with a survey of attitudes to attendance.  As a consequence of that feedback and new information received about social distancing measures in lecture theatres at the NHM, it was decided to cancel SVPCA this year.


For many of us the conference is an opportunity to socialize with people whom we only see once per year, and we won’t be able to do that via a digital conference. Thus we will not be running the conference virtually.
